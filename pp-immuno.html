IB Personal Project

Understanding how the immune system can protect, damage, fail, and modify disease.

Project Overview

The immune system is often described as the body's defense system. However, its role in disease is much more complex.

This project explores six major roles the immune system can play in disease, including both protective and harmful effects.

The Six Roles of the Immune System

1. Causing Tissue Damage

When immune responses become excessive or incorrectly target tissues, they can contribute to disease.

Examples coming soon.

2. Failing to Provide Enough Defense

When immune protection is insufficient, the body becomes vulnerable to infections and other threats.

Examples coming soon.

3. Responding to Pathogens

Immune responses against pathogens are essential for survival but can also create complications.

Examples coming soon.

4. Monitoring Abnormal Cells

The immune system helps identify and eliminate abnormal cells, including cancerous cells.

Examples coming soon.

5. Modifying Disease Processes

Immune activity can influence diseases that originally began through other mechanisms.

Examples coming soon.

6. Responding to Existing Damage

Immune responses can occur after initial damage and affect disease progression.
