IB Personal Project

Understanding how the immune system can protect, damage, fail, and modify disease.

Project Overview

The immune system is commonly described as the body's defense system. However, its relationship with disease is much more complex.

This project explores six possible roles of the immune system in disease and organizes diseases based on their primary immune mechanisms and secondary biological tags.

Research Framework

Each disease case will be analyzed using:

  • Primary immune role
  • Secondary mechanism tags
  • Disease mechanism
  • Scientific evidence evaluation
  • Current research developments
  • Communication for different audiences

Project Development

The final Immuno Gallery will include disease classifications, scientific illustrations, educational materials, and an illustrated book designed for different levels of scientific knowledge.
