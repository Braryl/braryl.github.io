PP-Immuno Gallery

IB Personal Project

Understanding how the immune system can protect, damage, fail, and modify disease.

Project Overview

The immune system is commonly described as the body's defense system. However, its relationship with disease is much more complex.

This project organizes diseases according to six possible roles of the immune system, using primary immune roles and secondary mechanism tags.

Explore the Six Immune Roles

1. Immune-Mediated Damage

When immune responses intended to protect the body cause damage to healthy tissues.

Research Framework

Each disease case will be evaluated using:

  • Primary immune role
  • Secondary mechanism tags
  • Biological mechanism
  • Scientific evidence evaluation
  • Current research developments
  • Communication for different audiences

Final Product

The final Immuno Gallery will include disease classification pages, scientific illustrations, and an educational book designed for different levels of scientific understanding.
