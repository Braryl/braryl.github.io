Immune-Mediated Damage

When immune responses designed to protect the body cause damage to healthy tissues.

Concept Overview

The immune system protects the body by identifying and responding to threats. However, when immune responses become misdirected or excessive, they can damage normal tissues and contribute to disease.

This category explores diseases where immune activity itself becomes an important driver of tissue injury.

Disease Gallery
